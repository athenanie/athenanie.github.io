Hi! I'm Avery Nie, a second-year math student at the University of Toronto. I like making mathematical structures, particularly origami polyhedra. My favorite materials are 24 lb letter paper (modular origami), 60 lb cardstock (paper strip weaving), yarn (crochet), and pipe cleaners (take a wild guess). 

Browse the [portfolio](/portfolio.markdown) for pretty origami photos.

Outside of math and school, I like playing [violin](https://hhchamberstrings.sa.utoronto.ca/), piano, table tennis, and [card games](https://setwithforks-dev.web.app/).
